What can you add to an overdue business invoice?

Under the UK's late payment rules you can usually claim interest at 8% above the Bank of England base rate, plus fixed compensation. Enter the invoice and the base rate to see the figures.

Questions

Common questions

Which base rate should I use?

The rate in force on 30 June or 31 December before the payment was due. Bank Rate was 3.75% as of the Bank of England's 17 September 2026 decision (checked 21 October 2026); check the Bank of England's website for the current figure.

Does it apply to consumers?

No. It's for business-to-business invoices.

Is it legal advice?

No. GOV.UK's guidance on late commercial payments is the authority.
